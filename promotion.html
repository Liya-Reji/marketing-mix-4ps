Promotion – Branding & Communication Strategies

The college promotes its programs through print advertisements, education fairs, school outreach programs, and hoardings. Public relations activities include technical fests, cultural events, workshops, and national conferences.

The institution maintains a strong digital presence through its official website, social media platforms, and YouTube channel. Scholarships and referral programs also attract new students. Word-of-mouth marketing plays a major role in building trust.

  • Social media marketing
  • Newspaper advertisements
  • Education fairs & seminars
  • Scholarship promotions
