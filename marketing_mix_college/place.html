Place – Location & Service Delivery

Amal Jyothi College of Engineering is located in Kanjirappally, Kerala, in a peaceful and green campus environment that is ideal for focused learning. The campus is well-connected by road and easily accessible.

The college provides centralized service delivery through well-organized academic blocks, laboratories, library, hostels, and administrative offices. Digital services such as online portals, LMS platforms, and digital libraries improve learning convenience.

The college is located in a peaceful and accessible area connected by public transport. The campus layout supports smooth service delivery.

  • Easy road and bus access
  • On-campus hostels
  • Hybrid learning through LMS
